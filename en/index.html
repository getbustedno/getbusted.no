Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. You get 50 cards from the Original deck for free every night. Theme packs are one-time purchases, and Busted+ unlocks the whole app with one payment. No subscription.

Do we have to drink?

No. Turn on alcohol-free mode and every sip becomes a point. And skipping is always allowed.

How many can play?

From 2 to 30. Big groups get more cards that apply to everyone at once.

When is it out?

Soon on iPhone and Android. Follow @getbusted.no on Instagram and TikTok to hear first.
